# Collective-Intelligence / Epistemic Coordination Protocol — Candidate v1

This protocol is subordinate to current project binding, PRIMARY/MANAGER/RESEARCH authority separation, claims/leases/fencing, work holds, durable-state CAS, coordination publication, mutation authorization, and consequence gateways. Epistemic confidence, consensus, a model generation, or a research rendezvous never grants mutation authority.

Research and Manager executions publish compact append-only epistemic contributions through the existing project-authoritative coordination transport. They do not mutate accepted shared-model state. The Primary may accept a reconciled model only when the existing accepted-state and current human mutation authorization gates independently permit the write.

High-impact questions use blinded first pass followed by rendezvous. Before rendezvous opens, lanes receive the question, bounded scope, permitted evidence surfaces, constraints, and output contract but not peer conclusions. After threshold or bounded timeout, peer records become visible and are classified as SUPPORT, QUALIFY, CONTRADICT, DUPLICATE_ORIGIN, TEMPORAL_VARIANT, CONTEXT_VARIANT, or UNRELATED.

Source identity is distinct from source instance. Mirrored or derived instances collapse to one evidence origin for corroboration. Negative evidence distinguishes NOT_FOUND from ABSENT and records search scope, inspected surfaces, date, version context, and confidence.

Reconciliation merges evidence, collapses duplicate source origins, normalizes applicability, recalibrates claim confidence, classifies apparent contradictions, updates competing hypotheses, preserves minority findings and superseded provenance, records a monotonically increasing model generation, and recomputes the information-gain frontier. Manager reprioritization is advisory task coordination; protected cancellation/reassignment remains subject to canonical task ownership and authority rules.

Checkpoint v3 references compact epistemic deltas. It never embeds the full evidence graph and does not alter v2 readers. A v2 producer may continue emitting v2 until v3 adoption is explicitly promoted.
